Discovery Framework: From Intent to Outcome

Every Cantellat engagement begins with one of three fundamental questions. These questions are not diagnostic; they are transformational. They expose the gap between what an organization wants to achieve and what it can currently execute. They also clarify the specific outcome that Cantellat will deliver.

1
"What is a problem you have but cannot afford to solve?"

What We Are Looking For

Organizations typically have one or two critical challenges that have been deferred because the traditional approach to solving them is prohibitively expensive. These might include:

  • Legacy technology infrastructure blocking digital transformation
  • Fragmented data environment preventing AI adoption
  • Manual, error-prone processes that seem too complex to automate
  • Market expansion opportunities requiring capabilities you don't possess
  • Regulatory requirements demanding system overhaul

The Cantellat Intervention

We do not ask you to accept lower quality or compromise scope. We change the execution model. We leverage our integrated ecosystem and AI-native architecture to compress timelines and reduce capital requirements.

Traditional Approach: Quarters or years, $2-3M
Cantellat Approach: 8 weeks, $400-600K
Outcome: Operational system live, 55% reduction in manual work
Example: Real-Time Operational Intelligence Platform

Problem: Large logistics network lacks unified visibility across suppliers, fleets, and operations. Manual tracking across regions. No predictive capability. Traditional approach: Quarters or years, $2-3M. Cantellat approach: ServCrust operational platform with real-time monitoring, geospatial intelligence, and predictive logistics, 8-12 weeks, $400-600K. Outcome: Live operational visibility, improved business coordination, reduced costs through predictive optimization.

2
"What is your most expensive operation?"

What We Are Looking For

Every organization has operational processes that consume significant resources. These are often accepted as fixed costs. However, they are frequently the most automatable processes in the organization:

  • Manual reporting and reconciliation (weeks of work)
  • Customer support operations (large teams)
  • Merchant or customer onboarding (multi-step workflows)
  • Sales qualification and lead routing (manual processes)
  • Compliance and audit workflows (manual documentation)
  • Cloud infrastructure costs (suboptimal utilization)

The Cantellat Intervention

We identify the most expensive operation, measure the opportunity, and deploy AI-native systems to automate it. The result is structural cost reduction—typically 30-50%—while improving quality, speed, and accuracy.

Typical Outcome: 30-50% cost reduction
Capital Freed: $500K-$5M annually
Quality: Improved accuracy and speed
Example: Customer Support Automation

Current state: 50-person support team, $3M annual cost, 48-hour response time. Cantellat intervention: AI-powered support agents with knowledge management. Outcome: 40% cost reduction ($1.2M saved), 2-hour response time, 95%+ satisfaction.

3
"What is one capability you wish you had that would directly grow your revenue?"

What We Are Looking For

Organizations often identify new revenue opportunities but lack the execution capability to capture them. These are typically capabilities requiring specialized infrastructure, domain expertise, or market access:

  • Embedded finance expansion
  • Digital banking services
  • AI-powered customer acquisition
  • Advanced analytics and personalization
  • Market expansion to new geographies
  • Agentic workflow automation

The Cantellat Intervention

We build and deploy the specific capability you need, at machine speed, with a team that stays embedded to ensure success. Revenue impact is measured in weeks, not quarters or years.

Revenue Impact: $2M-$50M annually
Time to Revenue: 4-12 weeks
Deployment: Execution-ready teams embedded
Example: Digital Banking Launch

Opportunity: Launch digital banking services for SME segment, $10M+ TAM. Missing capability: No digital banking platform, no compliance expertise. Cantellat solution: Wow Finstack WOWBANK with BFSI consulting and GTM. Outcome: New market segment captured, 16-week deployment, revenue generation in month 4.
